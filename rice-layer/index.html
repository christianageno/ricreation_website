These numbers are estimates. The math assumes the rice cooks on the flat base (rim diameter minus 2 cm), dry rice weighs about 0.85 g per cm³, and the cooked grains swell 2.6× (round), 2.8× (Albufera) or 3× (Bomba). Broth is a starting point: add more if the rice is still hard when the liquid is gone. Aim for a layer between 0.8 and 1.6 cm.
